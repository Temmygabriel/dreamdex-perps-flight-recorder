i
Scenario estimates, not liquidation predictions.This first version models one hypothetical position; it is not an isolated-margin account. dreamDEX uses cross margin by default, where positions share account-level USDso collateral, so this result excludes other positions and shared account equity. Dollar signs are shorthand for USDso quote-unit estimates, not a promise of withdrawable USD. Maker/taker fees are documented as 0% on the current testnet, but this model excludes liquidation fees, gas and stop-order costs, spread/slippage, changing funding rates, and full protocol liquidation execution.

ON-CHAIN

SDK liquidation-price estimate: the selected market moves against the account while other market contributions are held constant. It is not a guarantee of liquidation execution. Stale marks or unavailable RPC data suppress the estimate rather than displaying zero.

HONEST BY DESIGN

What this model does — and doesn't.

Included

Single-position directional P&L, manually defined starting margin, estimated maintenance requirement, and an explicit optional funding assumption.

Not yet modelled

Cross-margin offsets, fees, liquidation execution, insurance/backstop/ADL, other open positions, and protocol-specific equity accounting.

Data status

The app will attempt a read-only market-data check. Manual scenarios remain available if the endpoint is unavailable.
